# WilmAI Home 0.3.0 Beta

The children's school days side by side and on the fridge, messages to several teachers, and each child's own colour. · Lasten koulupäivät rinnakkain ja jääkaapin ovessa, viestit usealle opettajalle ja jokaiselle lapselle oma väri.

- The week shows when the children start or finish school together (within a
  quarter of an hour), and a shirt marks PE days. An optional lesson, such as
  remedial teaching or a club, can be left out of the school day: pick it in
  the week and untick "Counts in the school day". It stays, but no longer
  stretches the day.
- The usual week: Week → Usual week draws each weekday's usual hours, a bar for
  each child, from every week of the timetable the app has read. "Print for
  the fridge" shows it as an A4 sheet first, in a spring, summer, autumn or
  winter look; the page stays white, so it's light on ink.
- PE days: with an AI on, it reads each subject's name once; without one, the
  subject's name decides. If it's wrong, change it at any lesson.
- The calendar: an event over several days shows once across the week, and an
  event read from a message can be removed from the calendar (Undo puts it
  back). With an AI on, the same event announced in several messages shows
  once.
- Messages: write to several people at once. Tick them in the list, or add
  more while writing; they see each other's replies unless you untick that.
  A draft can always be thrown away, and the teachers' subjects show beside
  their names.
- Settings → Children: a colour for each child (strong or light), a nickname,
  and an emoji beside the coloured dot, or in place of the initials if you
  like.
- Smaller things: the top bar fits four children, homework days with one
  child's homework look right again, and the sign-in page says "Wilma,
  reimagined."
- Many of these came from a parent's ideas. Thank you!
