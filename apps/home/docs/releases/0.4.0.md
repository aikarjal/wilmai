# WilmAI Home 0.4.0 Beta

Your passwords move to your computer's keychain. · Salasanasi siirtyvät tietokoneesi avainnippuun.

- The Wilma passwords the app remembers, and your OpenAI key, are now kept in
  your computer's keychain: the macOS Keychain, or Windows Credential Manager.
  Before, they were in the app's own data, scrambled but not encrypted.
- They move by themselves the first time 0.4.0 starts. There's nothing to do
  and nothing to approve, and you stay signed in. Settings says where they
  are. If the keychain can't be used, they stay where they were and the app
  tries again at its next start.
- Signing in with the WilmAI CLI's saved login is gone: the app keeps its own
  login. If you used it, the app asks for that Wilma's password once.
- The app and the WilmAI CLI (and assistants that use it) still share one
  Wilma session, so they don't log each other out, also when the CLI keeps
  its password in the keychain.
- Reset app also deletes what the app kept in the keychain.

Problems and ideas: https://github.com/aikarjal/wilmai/issues or feedback@wilm.ai
