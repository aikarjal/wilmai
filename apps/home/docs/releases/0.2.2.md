# WilmAI Home 0.2.2 Beta

Write to the school in Finnish or Swedish, and a Wilma that hides its bulletins no longer hides everything else. · Kirjoita koululle suomeksi tai ruotsiksi, eikä Wilma, joka piilottaa tiedotteensa, enää piilota kaikkea muuta.

- Writing to the school in Finnish or Swedish: with OpenAI on, Settings
  (Writing to the school) offers Suomi or Svenska; it's off until you choose.
  Then a new message and an answer show your words and what's sent side by
  side. Translate makes the translation, with the most accurate translator
  (about $0.003 a message), and a translation back into your language, so you
  can check what it says.
- Only what's shown is sent: change your words after translating, and Send
  waits until you translate again. A message already written in that language
  goes as you wrote it, and a switch by Send sends your own words instead.
- A Wilma that doesn't show a guardian its bulletins (Helsinki's) no longer
  stops the rest: the app read nothing from that Wilma ("Couldn't read …: Wilma
  HTTP 403 at /news"), and now it reads everything else as usual.
- Both follow rockaholiciam's pull request, which found the bulletins problem
  and had the idea of writing in Finnish or Swedish.

Problems and ideas: https://github.com/aikarjal/wilmai/issues or feedback@wilm.ai
