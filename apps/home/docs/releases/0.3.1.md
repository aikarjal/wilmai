# WilmAI Home 0.3.1 Beta

The AI reads more of Wilma for you, and you can set it right. · Tekoäly lukee Wilmasta enemmän puolestasi, ja voit korjata sen tulkinnat.

- Homework: with an AI on, it reads each homework text once: whether the class
  has something to do, and which part is only for pupils who were away. That
  part folds away wherever it is in the text and however the teacher puts it.
  "No homework" no longer disappears: it shows quietly under Given today and in
  the subject's history, and opening it lets you say it's homework after all.
- The Bring line in Homework leaves out lunch, breaks, assemblies, the class
  teacher's moment and clubs, however the school names them; with an AI on, it
  reads each subject's name once. If a lesson is wrong, change it at that
  lesson in the week ("On the Bring line in Homework").
- Grades: the averages know which report-card subjects are academic and which
  grade is behaviour, also when a school names them its own way. Open a
  subject to change what it counts as.
- Notes: click a note's type to choose what it shows under (praise, a remark,
  an absence…). It applies to every note of that type.
- Without an AI, the usual words of the names and texts decide, as before, and
  everything above can be changed by hand.
- The privacy page says what more goes to OpenAI with an AI on: the subject
  names on the report card, and the homework texts, without names.

Problems and ideas: https://github.com/aikarjal/wilmai/issues or feedback@wilm.ai
